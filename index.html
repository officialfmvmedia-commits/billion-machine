MODE 01 / 05

CHARGE

Chart calm or climbing? The machine does nothing loud. It stacks SOL. A full treasury is ammunition.

TRIGGER

ACTION

MODE 02 / 05

DEFEND

Price slips under its average past the threshold. The machine buys back — deeper dip, bigger buy — capped every cycle so it can never empty the tank at once.

TRIGGER
Price below MA by threshold
ACTION
Buyback, scaled to dip depth
LIMIT
Per-cycle spend cap

MODE 03 / 05

BURN

Everything the machine buys goes in one direction: out of existence. Burned on-chain, with the transaction on the receipt.

TRIGGER
Any tokens bought by the machine
ACTION
On-chain burn, every time
SUPPLY
Only goes down

MODE 04 / 05

IGNITE

Volume spikes and price prints a new high. The machine adds one small fuel buy to the fire — then cools down.

TRIGGER
Volume spike + new high
ACTION
One small fuel buy
LIMIT
Mandatory cooldown

MODE 05 / 05

UNLOCK

Clear a floor on the way to $1B and the machine fires a one-off event: a large burn, a holder payout, or a bigger buyback budget.

TRIGGER
Market cap clears a floor
ACTION
One-time unlock event
FREQUENCY
Once per floor. Ever.

    Rules you can read.
    Timing you can't.

    01

    Randomized timing

    Each action fires at a random moment inside its cycle. Bots can't sit on the hour and front-run it.

    02

    Cooldowns

    Every mode has a cooldown. No mode can fire back-to-back and drain the treasury in one move.

    03

    Published rules

    Thresholds, caps and floors are public. Everyone knows exactly how the machine thinks.
